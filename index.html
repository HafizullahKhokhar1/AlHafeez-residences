Architecture

A building that behaves like a landscape.

Stepped terraces, vertical timber fins and layered native planting soften the volume across every level. Deep overhangs filter afternoon sun while opening rooms to cross-breezes and garden views. Built to weather naturally, the facade shifts quietly from morning light to dusk.
